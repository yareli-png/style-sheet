Step by step

How to use this style sheet

1. Choose your setup
Pick your color scheme and mode: dark for most Netflix projects, light for docs and emails.
2. Browse and pick
Use the tabs above to find what you need: Colors for brand colors, Gradients for layered backgrounds, Typography for text styles.
3. Grab what you need
Hover a card and click the copy icon to grab a single value. To collect several, click cards to pin them to the collection tray.
4. Take it with you
Copy your styles as code or export a file. Either way, it's ready to drop straight into any AI tool.

FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions about using this style sheet.

Getting started
What are the named colors and styles?
Each color, gradient, and text style has a name, also called a token. Use the token name instead of memorizing exact codes. It keeps your work consistent and makes it easy to reference in prompts or handoffs.
I'm not a designer. Can I still use this?
Absolutely. This tool is for everyone at Netflix who makes visual content. No design background needed.
Schemes and modes
What's the difference between Core, Cool, and Warm?
Three schemes, each with a different feel. Core is the classic red look. Cool is blue-toned, for product and tech. Warm is orange-toned, for creator contexts. Pick one and stick with it.
When should I use Dark vs Light mode?
Dark for anything that looks like Netflix. Light for docs, emails, and internal tools. Not sure? Drop us a message on Slack!
Using the tool
How do I copy a color?
Hover over any card and click the copy icon. The value lands on your clipboard, ready to paste.
What is the pin bar at the bottom?
Your collection tray. Click cards to save them, then hit Copy CSS to get all your pinned styles as CSS variables, or Export .md to save a file you can share or attach directly to an AI prompt.
What is a .md file and how do I use it?
.md is Markdown, a plain text file that AI tools can read directly. Export one to get a compact file of your pinned styles. Attach it to any AI prompt and it'll automatically use the right colors, fonts, and tokens.
Colors and typography
Why are there different color categories?
Each one has a purpose. Primary for brand colors, Secondary for backgrounds, Bright for expressive accents, Text for type colors, and Status Colors for links, errors, success, and warnings. Using the right one keeps things consistent.
Can I use any color on this page?
Yes, everything here is approved. Just don't bring in colors from outside this set. Need something you don't see? Let us know!
What are the text styles for?
Each text style is ready to use: font, size, weight, and spacing all in one. Pin and Export it just like a color.
Support
Who should I contact with questions?
For any questions or assistance, reach out on Slack. Our visual designers will reply as soon as possible.
